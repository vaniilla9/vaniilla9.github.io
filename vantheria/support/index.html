Common questions

Where are my saves? Can I move them to a new device?

Vantheria keeps three save slots on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud and Sync saves with iCloud is on (Settings in the game), they also sync to your other iPhone or iPad. The most recent save wins.

My progress didn't sync

Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account on both devices, that iCloud Drive is turned on, and that the game's iCloud setting is on. Open the game on the device with the newer save first, then on the other one.

I keep dying to a boss

Each area shows a recommended level. Run a few expeditions, rest at the inn (it's free in the Hushwood), and try Story difficulty in Settings if you'd rather enjoy the tale. Healing and shields before the enemy's heavy attacks (shown in red) make a big difference.

Can I change difficulty or language?

Difficulty is in the game's Settings at any time. The game follows your iPhone's language (English, Spanish or German); to change only Vantheria's language, go to Settings › Apps › Vantheria › Language.

The animations make me queasy

Turn on Reduce Motion in Settings › Accessibility › Motion. Vantheria calms its battle effects and stills the world map when it's on.

Does the game have ads or purchases?

No. No ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases and no energy timers.
