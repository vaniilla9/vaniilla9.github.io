Vantheria

Seven lands, one blight, three endings.

A dark-fantasy, turn-based RPG for iPhone and iPad, built natively in SwiftUI. It began as a Discord RPG I ran for my community, and I rebuilt it from the ground up as a full mobile game with a seven-chapter story, tactical battles and a deterministic, fully tested engine.

~19,400lines of Swift
139automated tests
21classes
82enemies

The game

  • Tactical battles: see the enemy's next move, chain elemental combos, break guards
  • Choices that lead to one of three endings
  • Relics, loot, gear sets, crafting and a home village to rebuild
  • An endless roguelite tower, New Game+ and three difficulties

How it's built

  • A pure, deterministic engine: any battle can be replayed exactly in tests
  • A balance simulator where bot players run the real engine across many seeds
  • Data-driven content with every reference validated at launch
  • English, Spanish and German, iCloud saves, no third-party dependencies
