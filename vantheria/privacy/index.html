What we collect

Nothing. Vantheria does not collect, track, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking in the app.

Your saves

Your game saves are stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud and have the game's iCloud sync turned on, they're also stored in your own private iCloud storage so they can sync between your devices. Neither the developer nor anyone else can access them. You can delete a save at any time from the game's Settings, and deleting the app removes the saves on that device.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.
